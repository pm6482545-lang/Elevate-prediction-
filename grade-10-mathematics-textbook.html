About this Grade 10 Mathematics textbook

This Grade 10 Mathematics Learner's Textbook follows the Kenyan CBC curriculum and covers all three strands in one complete book. Every sub strand has clear lessons, worked examples, activities and further practice exercises, with answers at the back. It ends with five model test papers and their marking schemes, so learners can practise exactly the way they will be tested.

Ideal for Grade 10 learners, parents and Mathematics teachers looking for reliable CBC revision material.
